Update policy

Agents update themselves to the version of their ring. A ring either follows the latest version or stays on an approved version. Typical rollout: put one or two devices into test, leave early/stable on a fixed version and promote it when the new version works. Per device you can also pin a version or trigger an update manually (works while paused).
